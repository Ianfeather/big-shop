"Your account has been deleted" would then be a lie told by the system to the one person guaranteed to notice. It also cannot promise the Recipes are gone: whether they go depends on whether this was the account's last member, which the sequence decides inside a transaction that has not run yet when this is sent. No link back into the app. Every account-scoped route stops resolving the moment the gate closes, so a button here would lead somewhere broken. */}}
