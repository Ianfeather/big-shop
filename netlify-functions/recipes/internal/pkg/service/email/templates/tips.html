Three things Big Shop does that are easy to miss.

1. Paste a link, get a recipe

Most recipe sites will import straight into Big Shop — paste the URL and the ingredients, quantities and method come across. You can also photograph a page from a cookbook, or paste text in by hand.

2. The list adds up for you

Add three recipes that each want onions and you get one line for onions, not three. Quantities that cannot be sensibly combined stay separate, so nothing is quietly rounded or lost.

3. Share the account, not the list

Invite someone and you share recipes and a single shopping list. Whoever is at the shops sees what whoever planned the week put on it, as they put it on.
