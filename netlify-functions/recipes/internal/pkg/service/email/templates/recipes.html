Three things worth cooking this week, if you are short of ideas. Each is a weeknight dish with a short shopping list.

Pasta e ceci

Chickpeas, tinned tomatoes, garlic, rosemary, small pasta. Twenty-five minutes, almost entirely from the cupboard, and better the next day.

Roast chicken thighs with lemon and potatoes

One tin, one oven, forty minutes. The potatoes cook underneath the chicken and are the best part.

Dal with fried onions

Red lentils, turmeric, cumin, a lot of onions cooked slowly. Cheap, keeps for days, and freezes well.
